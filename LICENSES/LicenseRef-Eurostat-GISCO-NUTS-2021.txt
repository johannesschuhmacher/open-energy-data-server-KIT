LicenseRef-Eurostat-GISCO-NUTS-2021

This license reference covers the Eurostat/GISCO NUTS 2021 statistical units
data files included in this repository.

Source:
https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/gisco/geodata/statistical-units/territorial-units-statistics
https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/nuts/nuts-2021-metadata.pdf

The upstream metadata identifies the dataset as "Nomenclature of Territorial
Units for Statistics (NUTS) 2021 - Statistical Units - Data set" and identifies
European Commission, Eurostat (ESTAT), GISCO as the point of contact. The
metadata states that the dataset is based on EuroBoundaryMap v2020 from
EuroGeographics, Global Administrative Unit Layer 2015 country data from
UN/FAO, and data from the National Statistical Institute of Turkey.
